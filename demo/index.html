ORACLE OUTCOME

WHY THIS MATTERS

A judge can miss a real capture.

Recall measures how many oracle-positive responses the judge identifies. False negatives reveal successful attacks the judge missed.

Precision measures how often a positive verdict agrees with the oracle. False positives are positive verdicts without an oracle capture.

These are separate historical replay cohorts. They are not automatically paired with the selected attack experiment, and oracle disagreement is not a universal measure of vulnerability.

THE ENGINEERING

A reproducible evaluation pipeline.

RedAgent combines a Python attack engine with a research harness. The separation between generation, verification and judgment is the project’s central design choice.

PythonLiteLLMHTTP / JSONDeterministic oraclesGitHub Actions

Generate and adapt

Seed prompts and model-generated mutations test selected OWASP categories through an HTTP adapter.

Verify the outcome

Benchmark-specific checks determine whether a secret was captured. Completed runs are logged for analysis.

Audit the evaluator

Previously collected responses are replayed to LLM judges. Their verdicts are evaluated separately from attack generation.
